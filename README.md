Hi, I'm Daniel Púa (a.k.a. devploit), offensive security researcher from Cádiz, Spain. I lead security at [Magnific](https://www.magnific.com/) and spend the rest of my time breaking things for a living: bug bounty, penetration testing and code review across web, mobile and APIs. Everything I publish ends up at [devploit.dev](https://devploit.dev).

🧰 **Things I build and keep online**

- [JWTForge](https://jwtforge.com): decode, audit and break JWTs entirely in your browser. Six attack generators, zero requests sent.
- [pwny.cc](https://pwny.cc): a curated payload repository for security researchers.
- [x-utils](https://x-utils.com): twelve copy-paste console tools that pull metrics from X without paying for the API. Read-only, no server, no password.
- [nomore403](https://github.com/devploit/nomore403): bypass 403/40X responses. 1.9k+ stars, and the reason many of you are here.

🔎 **Research**

Assigned CVEs across WordPress plugins and the Node.js ecosystem (adm-zip, decompress, pathe), with public advisories and write-ups on the [blog](https://blog.devploit.dev). The up-to-date list, with CVSS scores and references, is at [devploit.dev/cves](https://devploit.dev/cves.html).

🏁 **Competitive security**

CTF player since 2017 with [SFPE](https://ctftime.org/team/227086), ex [ripp3rs](https://ctftime.org/team/50984). The full record of placements and podiums, solo and team, is at [devploit.dev/ctf](https://devploit.dev/ctf.html). Represented Spain in the European Cybersecurity Challenge (ECSC) and mentored the national team the year after. I also organise [Hack&Beers Málaga](https://hackandbeers.es/).

Same mindset in all of it: _If it parses, it breaks._ 🧨
